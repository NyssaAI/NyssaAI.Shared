# Licensing policy

NyssaAI.Shared uses the unmodified [PolyForm Small Business License 1.0.0](../LICENSE.txt).
The license text governs; this page summarizes the policy.

Company use qualifies when both conditions hold:

- Fewer than 100 employees and independent contractors in total.
- Revenue in the prior tax year below the inflation-adjusted equivalent of USD 1,000,000 in 2019, using the CPI measure specified in the license.

The company includes organizations controlling, controlled by, or under common control with it.
Revenue means the business's total revenue, not just revenue from a product using these libraries.
The prior tax year is a revenue measurement period, not a one-year trial or license expiration.
There is no separate limit on application users.

Businesses outside these limits need a separate paid commercial license before company use.
Start a licensing inquiry through the [repository's issues](https://github.com/NyssaAI/NyssaAI.Shared/issues).
Pricing and additional rights require a separate agreement; this page grants neither.

This is source-available software. Dependency licenses remain their own and are not replaced by this license.
The [official license](https://polyformproject.org/licenses/small-business/1.0.0) is included unchanged in the repository and NuGet packages.
