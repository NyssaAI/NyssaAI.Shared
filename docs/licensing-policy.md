# Licensing policy under preparation

This document records the intended business policy. It is not a license grant or a commercial agreement.

Commercial users must obtain a paid license if they exceed either 50 users or $1,000,000 in revenue.
The definition and measurement period for users, revenue, and related organizations must be settled
before these terms are applied to the libraries.

The intended public distribution is source-available. The [Open Source Definition](https://opensource.org/osd)
requires permission for business use without this kind of restriction.

[PolyForm Small Business 1.0.0](https://polyformproject.org/licenses/small-business/1.0.0) is a reference
for a size-limited commercial grant. Its existing limits count fewer than 100 employees and contractors
and revenue below an inflation-adjusted amount based on USD 1,000,000 in 2019. Those are different from
the intended NyssaAI thresholds, so the unmodified license does not express this policy.

The [license authors permit adaptation](https://github.com/polyformproject/polyform-licenses), provided
modified licenses remove their name and website references. Any adapted NyssaAI license needs its own
name and review by qualified licensing counsel before it is relied upon commercially.

The final terms must cover individual and noncommercial use, the exact commercial eligibility test,
redistribution, modifications, notices, patent rights, termination, and the separate paid license route.
Prices and commercial contract terms are separate from the public license.
